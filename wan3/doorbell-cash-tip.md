# Wan 3.0 Prompt: Doorbell Cash Tip (AI comedy sketch)

The exact prompt behind the doorbell-cam clip from the MatrixLab Instagram reel.
One character, one fixed camera, seven beats of dialogue in a single 15-second shot.

## Settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Model | Wan 3.0 |
| Mode | Generate (reference-to-video), audio on |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 |
| Duration | 15 s |
| Resolution | 720p |
| Prompt rewrite / extend | Off |

## Inputs you need

1. **Image 1 - opening frame (16:9):** your character standing in the hallway in front of the door camera, in the outfit and pose the video should start with, holding the delivery bag.
2. **Image 2 - face reference:** a clean, sharp face image of your character.
3. **Video 1 - motion reference:** a short clip that shows the movement and timing you want. It only drives action and timing, not the framing.

Upload the files in this order so the labels match. Replace "Lina" with your character's name and adjust hair, outfit and hallway details to match your Image 1.

## Prompt

```text
CORE SCENE
A continuous 15-second horizontal 16:9 doorbell video. A fixed wide-angle fisheye doorbell camera at an apartment front door looks slightly down a grey hallway with an American flag on the wall and orange markings on the floor. Flat overhead ceiling light, slightly washed-out colours, light digital noise. One adult woman, Lina, a food delivery driver with long platinum-blonde hair in a chocolate-brown sleeveless top and pants, stands in the hallway facing the door. The customer is a man standing behind the camera; his face is never shown. Only his hand and forearm enter from the bottom-left edge, and once, during the sniff, the back of his head and one shoulder.
Tone: a light comedy sketch. Lina's emotions are natural and readable, not exaggerated: small, clear facial reactions and body language, like a real person caught off guard.

REFERENCE ROLES
Image 1: the opening frame: 16:9 framing, fisheye doorbell look, hallway, flag, floor markings, lighting, her brown outfit, her starting pose and the brown paper bag in her left hand. Begin the video exactly on Image 1.
Image 2: Lina's face and hair: face shape, hazel eyes, light freckles, long platinum-blonde centre-parted wavy hair. Keep her identical in every frame.
Video 1: motion and timing only: her standing and talking toward the door camera, the brief glance away and her arm reaching toward the camera. Do not copy its vertical framing, the original woman's face, any phone in her hand, its voices or any on-screen text.

CAMERA LOCK
Shot 1 for the whole clip: fixed fisheye doorbell camera at the door frame, slightly high angle, wide framing with Lina's full body right of centre. No pan, zoom, push-in, shake or cut at any time.

BEATS
B1 GREETING (0-2s). Action: Lina smiles and greets the camera, bag in her left hand. Emotion: friendly, routine. Must be visible: her smile and mouth speaking.
B2 HANDOVER (2-4s). Action: she holds the bag out toward the camera; the man's hand comes in from the bottom-left edge, grips the folded top and pulls the bag out of frame. Emotion: polite. Must be visible: the bag passing from her hand into his hand before it leaves the frame.
B3 NO TIP (4-5.5s). Action: she tilts her head and points out the missing tip. Emotion: polite but pointed, a small raised eyebrow and a tight smile. Must be visible: the eyebrow and head tilt.
B4 OFFER AND REACTION (5.5-10s). Action: she listens to his offer, frowns, glances away down the hallway, lets out a short disbelieving laugh, looks back and agrees with a shrug. Emotion: confused, then amused disbelief, then "fine, whatever". Must be visible: the frown, the glance away, the shrug.
B5 SNIFF (10-12s). Action: she hesitantly holds out her right hand, back of the hand up; his hand takes her wrist, his head and shoulder lean in from the bottom-left edge with his face turned away from the camera, and he presses his nose to the back of her hand and sniffs twice. Emotion: awkward, slightly grossed out; she leans back a little and grimaces. Must be visible: the back of his head at her hand; must be audible: two loud sniffs.
B6 PUNCHLINE (12-14s). Action: his head pulls back out of frame while he admits he has no money; her face drops, she pulls her hand free and wipes it once on her pants. Emotion: stunned, then flat annoyance. Must be visible: the face drop and the wipe.
B7 EXIT (14-15s). Action: she rolls her eyes, says her last line, turns and walks away to screen-left. Emotion: done, deadpan. Must be visible: the eye-roll and the turn.

DIALOGUE
U1 (B1): Lina; English; on-camera lip-sync; exact line: "Hi, Uber Eats for Nocky?" 0.3-1.6s, friendly and upbeat. Say once.
U2 (B2): Man, off-screen behind the camera; English; voice only; exact line: "Appreciate it, thank you." 2.3-3.3s, casual, close to the microphone. Say once.
U3 (B3): Lina; English; on-camera lip-sync; exact line: "I noticed you didn't leave a tip boy." 3.8-5.3s, polite but pointed. Say once.
U4 (B4): Man; English; voice only; exact line: "If you let me sniff your hand, I'll give you a hundred dollars cash right now." 5.6-9.0s, quick, hopeful, a little awkward. Say once.
U5 (B4): Lina; English; on-camera lip-sync; exact line: "Really? Okay." 9.2-10.2s, a disbelieving laugh on "Really?", then a resigned "Okay." Say once.
U6 (B6): Man; English; voice only; exact line: "Yeah... I don't actually have a hundred dollars." 12.2-13.5s, sheepish, slower. Say once.
U7 (B7): Lina; English; on-camera lip-sync; exact line: "I'm so done with Uber Eats." 13.7-14.9s, flat and deadpan. Say once.
No speech between 10.2s and 12.2s: this is the sniff.

SECOND-BY-SECOND TIMELINE
[0-1s] Visual: B1. Lina stands exactly as in Image 1, bag hanging from her left hand, right arm relaxed; she looks into the lens with a friendly smile and starts speaking. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U1 begins at 0.3s in Lina's voice; soft hum of the ceiling lights, no music.
[1-2s] Visual: B1. She finishes her greeting with a small nod and takes one step toward the door, still smiling at the lens, bag in her left hand. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U1 ends at 1.6s; one footstep and ceiling-light hum, no music.
[2-3s] Visual: B2. She lifts the bag toward the camera with her left hand; the man's hand enters from the bottom-left edge and grips the bag's folded top; for a moment both hands hold the bag. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U2 begins at 2.3s from the man behind the camera; paper rustle, no music.
[3-4s] Visual: B2. She lets go; his hand pulls the bag down and out of frame at the bottom-left edge; she lowers her left arm, her smile fading into a polite expression. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U2 ends at 3.3s; U3 begins at 3.8s in Lina's voice; paper rustle fades, no music.
[4-5s] Visual: B3. She tilts her head slightly, raises one eyebrow and speaks with a tight, polite smile, hands relaxed at her sides, eyes on the lens. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U3 continues and ends at 5.3s; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[5-6s] Visual: B4. She holds the eyebrow raised and waits for his answer, arms folded loosely across her waist, eyes on the lens. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U4 begins at 5.6s from the man; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[6-7s] Visual: B4. As she hears "sniff your hand" her brows pull together into a confused frown and her head moves back slightly, lips parted. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U4 continues from the man without restarting; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[7-8s] Visual: B4. She glances away down the hallway to screen-left and lets out a short disbelieving breath through her nose, the corner of her mouth twitching into a smile. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U4 continues from the man; her soft snort of disbelief, no music.
[8-9s] Visual: B4. She looks back at the lens with an amused half-smile, unfolding her arms, weighing the offer. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U4 continues and ends at 9.0s; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[9-10s] Visual: B4. She laughs once in disbelief on "Really?", then shrugs her shoulders and nods with a resigned smile on "Okay." Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U5 from 9.2s to 10.2s in Lina's voice; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[10-11s] Visual: B5. She hesitantly holds out her right hand toward the camera at chest height, back of the hand up, arm not fully stretched, her face a little wary; his hand enters from the bottom-left edge and takes her wrist. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: No dialogue; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[11-12s] Visual: B5. The back of his head and one shoulder lean in from the bottom-left edge, face turned away from the camera, and his nose presses against the back of her hand; she leans back slightly with a small grimace, eyes wide, lips pressed together. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: No dialogue; two loud, drawn-out sniffs from the man close to the microphone, no music.
[12-13s] Visual: B6. His head pulls back out of frame at the bottom-left edge while his hand still holds her wrist; her grimace freezes, then her face drops into a stunned stare at the lens. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U6 begins at 12.2s from the man; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[13-14s] Visual: B6. She pulls her hand free, wipes the back of it once on her pants and exhales, jaw set in flat annoyance; his hand disappears out of frame. Camera: Shot 1 locked. Audio: U6 ends at 13.5s; U7 begins at 13.7s in Lina's flat voice; ceiling-light hum, no music.
[14-15s] Visual: B7. She rolls her eyes, finishes her line and turns away, taking one step down the hallway toward screen-left, face in three-quarter view; hold on her walking away through the final frame. Camera: Shot 1 locked through the final frame. Audio: U7 ends at 14.9s; one footstep and ceiling-light hum, no music.

CONTINUITY AND EXCLUSIONS
One continuous take. Lina is the only fully visible person; the man's face is never shown. One brown paper bag, handed over once in B2 and gone after second 4; Lina holds no phone and no cash. Each line is spoken once by its named speaker, word for word. Emotions stay natural and small: no cartoon faces, no screaming, no slapstick. No on-screen text, captions, subtitles, no date or time stamp, no camera UI, logos or watermark, no music.
```

## Why it works

- **Each reference has one job.** Opening frame, identity and motion are separated, so the model does not mix them up.
- **A camera lock** keeps the fixed doorbell look for all 15 seconds: no drift, no cuts.
- **Beats with "must be visible" checks** turn a comedy sketch into reactions the model actually shows.
- **Timed dialogue lines (U1-U7)** give each speaker one exact line in a fixed window, so lip-sync and the punchline land.
